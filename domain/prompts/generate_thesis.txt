1. Directional Balance (Bullish or Bearish): An investment thesis can be BULLISH (Long) or BEARISH (Short).

6. Initial Strength: Assign an initial strength score between 0.20 and 0.85 representing how strongly the current evidence supports this thesis:
   - High strength (0.65-0.85) for confirmed, direct, unpriced catalysts.
   - Low or moderate strength (0.20-0.45) for emerging risks, indirect developments, or situations with heavy counter-evidence.
